# Glossary

**Audit log.** tScore's record of every change to an event: what changed, when and why. Old
scores are kept there when a card is edited.

**Card.** The punched scorecard a rider carries for one lap. Observers punch the score for each
section.

**Card label.** The sticky label on a card's header saying whose card it is and which lap, with
a QR code.

**Class.** A group of riders who ride the same sections and are placed against each other, such
as a grade or a junior class.

**Clean.** A score of 0: the section ridden with no dab.

**Competition status.** Whether a rider is competitive (placed), no award (scored, not placed)
or non-competitive (listed only). See [Riders](before-the-event.md#riders).

**Dab.** Putting a foot down. One dab scores 1, two score 2, three or more score 3.

**DNF.** Did not finish: the rider started but retired.

**DNS.** Did not start: the rider entered but didn't ride.

**Event code.** A short code for the event, printed in each label's QR code.

**Fail.** A score of 5: for example a stop, a fall, or leaving the section's boundaries.

**Gate trial.** A trial scored by gates ridden; the highest total wins.

**Lap.** One time round all the sections. Each lap has its own card.

**Minder.** A helper accompanying a junior rider. Entered, but not scored and given no labels.

**Observer.** The official at a section who watches each rider and punches their card.

**Overdue.** Later than the class's average lap by more than the margin set in Event setup. Only
a prompt to look: it never marks anyone DNF.

**Penalty.** Points added to a rider's total for something other than a section score, such as
course cutting.

**Provisional results.** Results while any card is missing or any score undecided. Places may
still change.

**Revision.** A numbered version of the final results. Correcting them after they are final
makes the next revision.

**Ride-off.** A short extra contest to split riders who are exactly level.

**Second class.** A class a rider also enters, keeping the same number, so their scores count
in both.

**Section.** One marked-out obstacle course. A lap is made of several sections.
