# Code of conduct

tScore adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

## In short

We want tScore to be a welcoming project for everyone: riders, scorers, club officials and
developers, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, focus
on what is best for clubs and the community.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; use sexualised language or imagery; make personal
or political attacks; publish anyone's private information (including riders' details) without
explicit permission; or behave in ways that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub (Security → Report a
vulnerability, marked "Code of conduct"), or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are
handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban anyone, following the Contributor Covenant 2.1
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
