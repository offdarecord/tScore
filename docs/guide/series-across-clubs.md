# A series run by several clubs

It's common for a few clubs to share a series: for example, three clubs running the five rounds
of a winter series between them. Each club scores its own rounds on its own laptop, so the
results start out on three different laptops. This page is how to bring them together for the
series points.

There's no internet link between laptops: each round travels as one file, by USB stick, email or
a shared drive.

## Agree before the first round

The series tally matches riders and classes by name across every round, so the clubs need to use
the same words:

- **Class names, exactly the same** at every round: "Trial 4 Plus" at one round and "Trial 4+"
  at another make two different classes in the tally. Agree the list in writing before the
  first round. A club that has opened another club's round file (below) can also use
  **Copy from a previous event…** on Event setup to start from its classes.
- **Riders' names as they enter them.** Riders are matched by name, so a rider entered as "Sam"
  at one round and "Samuel" at another gets two lines. Rider numbers can differ between rounds:
  they don't matter to the tally.
- **One series keeper**: the person (and laptop) who keeps the series and publishes the tally.
  The points scale, best-rounds rule and tie rule are set once, on the keeper's laptop (see
  [Series points](series.md#rules)).

## After each round

At the club that ran the round:

1. Check the results and **Mark results final** (see
   [Mark results final](results.md#mark-results-final)). Only final rounds count.
2. On **Event setup**, under **Saving and backup**, choose **Save a copy for another laptop…**.
   This saves the whole event as one `.tscore` file, checked as it's saved. Name it so the keeper
   can tell the rounds apart, for example `Winter Series Rd 2 - Example MCC.tscore`.
3. Send the file to the series keeper, on a USB stick, by email or on a shared drive. It holds
   the riders' names, clubs and scores, and the card photos, so share it only with the clubs
   running the series.

The club keeps scoring and correcting on its own laptop: the file is a copy.

## On the series keeper's laptop

1. On Home, choose **Open event file…** and pick the round's `.tscore` file. tScore checks the
   file is complete and opens it as an event on this laptop. Don't score or change anything in
   it: it's the club's round, here only for the tally.
2. Go to **Series points**, open the series, and add the round from **Add a round from your
   events**.
3. Save the tally as a PDF or CSV to publish (see
   [Save the tally to share](series.md#save-the-tally-to-share)).

The keeper's own rounds are added the same way, straight from **Add a round from your events**.

## When a round is corrected

If a club reopens a round to correct something and marks it final again:

1. The club saves and sends a new `.tscore` file, as above.
2. The keeper opens it. tScore opens it as a new event alongside the old copy (with "(2)" after
   its folder name), so nothing is overwritten.
3. In the series, the keeper removes the old copy of the round and adds the new one, then saves
   the tally again.
4. The old copy can then be deleted from **Manage events**.

## Good to know

- **Every laptop needs the same version of tScore**, or at least as new as the laptop that saved
  the file. An older tScore can't open an event saved by a newer one, and says to update.
- **Every club can keep the tally too.** The keeper sends each round's file to every club; each
  club adds the rounds to its own copy of the series. It's the same steps on every laptop.
- **Sending only the results isn't enough.** The tally reads the round's final results from the
  event itself, so a results PDF or CSV can't be added as a round.
