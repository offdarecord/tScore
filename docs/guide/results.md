# Results

**Results** shows each class on its own tab, worked out from every card in so far. It updates
as cards are saved, so you can watch it fill in during the day.

## Provisional and final

While any card is missing or any score is undecided, results are **provisional**. The top of
the screen says what's missing (for example "2 cards still missing. Positions may change."):
choose **See what's missing** for the list. Riders waiting for a lap say so in their row.

When every card is in, it says **All cards are in**. Check the results, then mark them final.

## Ranking and ties

Within each class:

1. **Competitive** riders are placed first, then **No award** (NA) riders ranked among
   themselves, then **Non-competitive** (NC) riders, who are listed with their counts only.
2. The lowest total wins (the highest, for a gate trial). Penalties are included in the total.
3. Equal totals are split by the most cleans (0s), then the most 1s, 2s and 3s, then the fewest
   5s. The line under each table says the rule for this event.
4. Riders still level after all of that share the place. Recording a ride-off comes in a later
   version.

A rider in a second class appears in both, marked "Also in".

## Saving results to share

Choose **Save PDF…** to print, email or pin up, or **Save CSV…** for a spreadsheet or a website.
Files are saved in the event's `exports` folder unless you choose somewhere else; **Open** opens
the one just saved. Provisional results print **PROVISIONAL** at the top with what's missing;
final ones say **FINAL**.

Tick **Show junior riders' surnames as an initial** to print "Riley M." for riders in junior
classes. It only changes saved files: results on this laptop always show full names.

## Mark results final

When the results are checked, choose **Mark results final…**, then **Mark final**. This makes a
numbered revision (revision 1, then 2 if they're corrected later). While results are final,
cards, penalties, riders and the setup can't change, so nothing can move a place by accident.
Save the PDF again afterwards: it now says FINAL.

## Reopen to correct something

If a mistake turns up after results are final:

1. Choose **Reopen…**, say what needs correcting, and choose **Reopen results**. The reason is
   logged, and printed on the next revision.
2. Make the correction (for example on [Manual entry](event-day.md#correct-a-card)). tScore
   shows which places have changed since the last revision.
3. Mark the results final again. They become the next revision.

## Closing the event

When [Still on course](event-day.md#still-on-course) shows everyone accounted for and the
results are final, the event is done. Go back with **← All events**: if anything changed since the
last backup, tScore makes one more as the event closes. Take the USB stick with the second copy home with you.
