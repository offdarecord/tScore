# Try a test trial

Before tScore's first real event, rehearse the whole day at home with the **Test trial kit**. It
takes about an hour and checks that your laptop, camera, printer and labels work together, with
nothing real at stake.

Download `tScore-Test-Trial-<version>.zip` from the
[latest release](https://github.com/offdarecord/tScore/releases/latest) and unzip it. It has:

- `README - start here.html`: the steps, one page, to print or keep open.
- `entry-list.xlsx`: 12 invented riders in Trial 2, 3 and 4, in the Entryplace format.
- `practice-cards.pdf`: 24 practice cards at true size (43 × 145 mm), four to a page, with what
  to punch on each card under them.
- `answer-key.json`: what was punched, for tScore to check against.

## The steps

1. **Make the event:** **New event**, named `TEST TRIAL Practice Event` with the code `TEST`.
   Set **Trial 2**, **Trial 3** and **Trial 4** to 6 sections per lap and 2 laps.
2. **Import** `entry-list.xlsx` on **Riders**.
3. **Print the labels** on **Card labels** (Avery L7651, or plain paper for a test).
4. **Print the practice cards** at **100% (actual size)**, on light card if you can, and check
   the 100 mm line with a ruler. Cut them out.
5. **Punch the scores** listed under each page's cards, or fill each circle in solidly with a
   black marker. Stick each lap label on the card it names.
6. **Scan the cards** on **Scan desk**, and decide anything in the **Review queue**.
7. On **Results**, choose **Check a test trial…** and pick `answer-key.json`. tScore says how
   many cards it read correctly, names any wrong scores, and suggests what to look at: glare,
   focus, framing, or the punch itself. Then mark the results final and save the PDF and CSV.

**Check a test trial…** shows only for events with "TEST TRIAL" in their name. When you're done,
delete the event from **Manage events** on the home screen.

The practice cards are a generic tScore card, not a copy of any club's card. They have the same
layout tScore reads: the black section column, rows 1 to 16, and 0 1 | 2 3 5.
