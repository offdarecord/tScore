Test trial: rehearse the day at home

TEST TRIAL  For tScore {{VERSION}}. About an hour. Invented riders only: nothing here is a real event.

This kit takes you through a whole event with {{RIDERS}} invented riders, {{LAPS}} laps of {{SECTIONS}} sections: import the entries, print labels, scan {{CARDS}} practice cards, and mark results final. At the end tScore checks what it read against the answer key, so you know your laptop, camera and printer are ready before a real event.

You need: tScore installed, the document camera and black mat, an A4 printer, light card (or plain paper), scissors, and a card punch or a black marker. Labels (Avery L7651) are optional: plain paper works for a test.
  1. Make the event 5 minutes In tScore, choose New event. Name it {{EVENT_NAME}} and give it the code {{EVENT_CODE}}. In Classes, set Trial 2, Trial 3 and Trial 4 to {{SECTIONS}} sections per lap and {{LAPS}} laps. Choose Create event.
  2. Import the entries 2 minutes On Riders, choose Import from entry list… and pick entry-list.xlsx from this kit. It's in the Entryplace format, so this tests the real import. Check the preview, then import {{RIDERS}} riders.
  3. Print the labels 5 minutes On Card labels, print the labels on Avery L7651 sheets, or on plain paper and cut them out.
  4. Print and cut the practice cards 15 minutes Print practice-cards.pdf at 100% (actual size), not "fit to page", on light card if you can. Measure the 100 mm line on any page: if it's not 100 mm, the printer scaled it, and the cards won't read the same as real ones. Cut out the {{CARDS}} cards along their outlines.
  5. Punch the scores 15 minutes Under each page's cards is what to punch on them. For each section, punch the hole for its score, or fill that circle in solidly with a black marker. Stick each rider's lap label on the card it names.
  6. Scan the cards 10 minutes On Scan desk, choose the camera and put each card on the black mat. Clear cards save themselves; anything unclear goes to the Review queue. Decide those there, just as on the day.
  7. Results 5 minutes On Results, choose Check a test trial… and pick answer-key.json from this kit. tScore says how many cards it read correctly, and names any wrong cells, with what to look at (glare, focus, framing, or the punch itself). Then mark the results final and save the PDF and CSV, to see the whole day through.
If cards are read wrongly: check the light (no glare on the card), the camera height (the whole card in view, with a margin), and that focus settles before the card is read. Fix one thing, rescan the cards that were wrong (edit them on Manual entry or scan again), and check again.

When you're done, delete the event from Manage events on the home screen. Questions: see the tScore user guide, or tell the maintainers at github.com/offdarecord/tScore/issues.
